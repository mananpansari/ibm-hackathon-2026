# Result 3: the ladder vs simpler policies at equal officer capacity

Generated by `python sweep.py`. Chart: `docs/result3.svg` (scam rate 0.5%).

**Real:** payments of >= 10 USDT to the Jul-Oct 2026 test wallets (Tether-frozen = scam, comparison = legitimate), their amounts, senders and order, and Tether freezes. Payments are packed into 30 equal bank days so each officer-day carries a realistic load.

**Modelled (not measured):** scam rate (swept); coached victim confirms the script 50%, legitimate customer 2%; scam stopped at final tier {'release': 0.0, 'warning': 0.05, 'question': 0.25, 'hold': 0.8}; no after-the-fact customer reports. Fixed threshold = first-time payee >= 10,000 USDT. Officers call every hold left in a slot at the end of its day and mark the wallet confirmed or cleared, correctly 90% of the time.

**Read with care:**

- Most legitimate payments here go to the volume-matched comparison wallets, which were chosen to look like frozen wallets in payment size. Real customer traffic has fewer large first-time payees, so the share of legitimate customers asked a question is likely overstated.
- Only the ladder has a question tier, so 'asked' (friction) has no counterpart in the baselines.
- One random seed per cell: small differences between neighbouring capacities are noise.
- Amounts are USDT (about S$1.3 each); none of this counts victims, only payments.
